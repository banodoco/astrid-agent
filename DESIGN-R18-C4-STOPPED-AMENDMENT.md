## 3. Positive conjunction and absence semantics

Define named predicates for review, not accepting Boolean API fields:

```text
C4_ADMIT = PREPARED_STOPPED_AND_SUPPRESSED
        AND REAL_BOUND_HOST_REBOOT
        AND NO_ASTRID_INVOCATION_THIS_BOOT
        AND CLEAN_COMPLETE_SPAWN_AUDIT
        AND RETAINED_ORIGINAL_OWNER_NB
        AND EFFECTIVE_CURRENT_LAUNCH_FENCE
        AND P1 AND P2 AND P3_C4 AND P4 AND P5 AND P6 AND P7 AND P8
```

Each operand must have a known positive value, trustworthy provenance, applicable interval and independent verifier. The three-valued evaluation is: every required operand known true permits consideration of admission; any known false refuses; any unknown/unsupported refuses. A parser's `observed` means collection returned data, not that this conjunction is true. A matching digest of a known false or unknown value is not a pass. Preserve W's result/code/fault/FieldCheck invariants and every fault, with `recovery_required` after owned or uncertain effects.

The unit name alone is not an installation identity. Bind UID, user-manager namespace, host/kernel/proc identity, exact unit aliases, installed interpreter/module/manifest/realm and the audited route universe. A fake same-name user service or another UID's journal cannot satisfy a field. C4 makes no claim against arbitrary hostile same-user code that bypasses advisory locks, ptraces the updater or forges the installed environment; D:113's trusted installed-code/user boundary remains. Ordinary concurrent editors, stale records and independent replacement still require detection/preservation and must not be recast as trusted cooperation.

**Why the reboot matters:** a real new host kernel cannot retain the old kernel's running R0 descendants. That eliminates the need to recover their unwitnessed original-launch history. It says nothing about R0 processes launched **after** the reboot. Whole-boot no-service-invocation plus a complete alternate-route audit and effective current exclusion must supply that second half. If those are not provable, the argument is incomplete even when the lock is free.

The definition of “clean” is bound to the reviewed authentic launch universe, not a claim that every arbitrary executable on the machine was inspected. Product direct openers remain governed by the original lock, but that lock does not retroactively prove no earlier direct opener spawned a surviving non-lock-holding descendant. A route/profile unable to exclude that case cannot use P3_C4. No directory scan or clock bracket repairs this history gap.

## 4. Trustworthy provenance and source limits

The trusted producers must be installed, source-bound code under the existing installation authority. Agent derives and pins selected configuration and collects native facts; Runtime independently authenticates its actual live connections, verifies native facts and holds the real lock. Repeating Agent bytes, reading an Agent-signed/HMACed file, root UID, a PID match or a nonce is not an independent native observation. Hashes bind content; they do not authenticate the native handler or establish a continuous negative history.

Carry preparation across reboot only as a durable **pending operation record**, not as a live `ManagerEvidence`, `VerifiedEvidence`, Seal or lease. The record must bind at least: original operation and outer update-job mapping; preboot attempt/generation; authentic source/selection/realm/root and owner-lock IDs; exact prior stopped/enabled/load policy and configuration/preimages; genuine preboot boot_id and host/kernel/proc identity; owned guard directory/drop-in/active-object IDs and bytes; durable install intent and readback; audit inventory/profile/transcripts/errors; and the intended same-operation continuation. Protect it with pinned no-follow traversal, expected owner/mode/ACL, durable write and directory fsync. Torn, missing, substituted, cloned, replayed or foreign records refuse and preserve pending recovery. A file hash alone cannot establish the truth of its recorded preboot native state or its continuity.

Postboot, independently revalidate the record's installed source, binding and actual guard/native configuration, and collect current kernel/manager/audit facts through fresh authenticated channels. Runtime recomputes all required checks and pins complete transcripts in its private evidence store. It must know why the preboot record is from the installed authorized producer and unchanged since that preparation; this task supplies no new attestation authority. If the existing trust boundary/storage cannot establish the needed provenance, return unsupported, not a new root helper or self-certified witness. Public receipts may expose digests and failures; private paths, capabilities and full preimages stay private.

Two documented API limits make the absence requirement substantive:

- The kernel documents boot_id as a UUID that remains fixed after its first read. A separately read, genuine host-kernel value supports the boundary comparison; the comparison does not authenticate a saved value or a different machine. [Kernel sysctl documentation](https://www.kernel.org/doc/html/latest/admin-guide/sysctl/kernel.html#random).
- The systemd 255 unit interface exposes InvocationID and current unit state, not an authenticated complete all-boot absence assertion. Treat it as a source of current/counterexample facts; this design does not infer historical nonexecution from it. [systemd 255 D-Bus interface](https://www.freedesktop.org/software/systemd/man/255/org.freedesktop.systemd1.html).
- Journal visibility depends on access and storage configuration. Journald permits disabled storage, rate limits and retention limits; a successful empty query cannot certify total coverage. Relevant manager/system records can also differ from the service's own records. A collector must establish exact boot/user/unit coverage and completeness independently before treating a journal search as more than corroboration. No such completeness mechanism is supplied here. [journalctl 255](https://www.freedesktop.org/software/systemd/man/255/journalctl.html), [journald.conf 255](https://www.freedesktop.org/software/systemd/man/255/journald.conf.html).

The inherited-listener counterexample from 41616314137 remains applicable: peer pidfd/SO_PEERCRED and a matching separately reported MainPID do not establish the actual request handler. Reboot is not an authentication mechanism. Neither this packet nor a root test harness closes that AG1/Runtime-verifier gap.

## 5. Ordering, durable preparation, and fences

### A. Prepare in boot B0, while the authentic selected R0 is stopped

1. Reconcile every unfinished/release-pending operation before fetch or a new operation. Bind sources, selection, installed unit, host/kernel and prior stopped/enabled/load state. Obtain the existing Agent installation/operation authority and Runtime bootstrap authority in D's order. Initial observations are read-only; a live/unknown R0 refuses with the existing message: “Astrid is still running. Choose Pinokio's Stop button, then try again.” No updater-issued stop or kill.
2. Obtain a fresh control session, pin the original directory and owner.lock and acquire NB provisional custody. Validate stopped selection/native state and P1/P2/P7 facts needed to prepare. This is **not HELD_ADMITTED**, does not require claiming old descendant absence and authorizes no DB open, backup, migration, selection switch or candidate launch. If any required preparation fact is unavailable, stop before suppression effects.
3. Persist suppression intent/preimages. Publish only create-only owned objects: persistent private active object first, then the independent unit drop-in. Use `O_CREAT|O_EXCL|O_NOFOLLOW` and complete durable write before a manager reload, or a fully written/fsynced private staged file published with `renameat2(RENAME_NOREPLACE)` where supported. Existing leaf/symlink/hardlink or unknown parent ownership is refusal. A crash leaving a partial file is recovery, never an effective gate. Do not rewrite R0/user fragment or drop-in bytes, change their symlinks, reset their conditions, disable their activation links or restore over an independent edit to make this work.
4. The proposed independent drop-in is `[Unit] ConditionPathExists=!<persistent-private-active-object>` plus `[Service] Restart=no`, as in D:215. Do not reset any preexisting condition list. Check full effective configuration: precedence, possible condition resets/OR semantics, aliases, generated/transient overrides, dependencies and environment. Filename ordering is not authority. The negated condition blocks startup only while that path exists; the path must survive reboot and be visible before the first relevant startup. This meaning follows [systemd.unit 255](https://www.freedesktop.org/software/systemd/man/255/systemd.unit.html); behavior on the actual supported native manager still needs proof.
5. Reload only the authenticated selected manager, independently verify loaded denial, no old process/pending jobs and exact owned changes, and complete the preboot route audit. A foreign change returns `binding_changed`; uncertain effects retain suppression if safely possible and `recovery_required`. This task does not claim daemon-reload is an atomic compare-and-mutate operation. If that exclusion cannot be provided, the strict route remains unsupported.
6. Fsync a same-operation “awaiting user restart” preparation checkpoint after effective denial/audit readback. Read B0 from the genuine kernel; record scope and trust provenance. Leave suppression in place. Close provisional handles only as an explicit, recorded end of this **unadmitted preparation** after the persistent gate is verified; this grants no data permission. A reboot will destroy all handles in any case. Never describe this as uninterrupted crossboot custody or an unlock/reacquire writer handover. The pending preparation must block normal activation/new updates. It is not a completed or rolled-back operation, and it must not trigger ordinary rollback cleanup that removes the gate.
7. The user restarts the operating system once. No automatic reboot or attempt to stop an unknown service is implied. The preparation wait is durable and resumable; it is not a live IPC/start deadline stretched across reboot. Further reboots do not automatically authorize replay or mint a new operation; reconcile and re-establish the entire conjunction against the recorded preparation.

### B. Continue the same operation in boot B1

1. Resolve the pending preparation before fetch/new intent. Authenticate the preparation's binding and guard objects, compare genuine B1 to B0, validate persistent effective denial from the first possible postboot service activation, and independently establish no service invocation and clean route coverage for B1. Current presence cannot prove the gate was never removed/replaced earlier; absent continuity evidence is refusal. A same-boot continuation can report “restart still required” but cannot proceed or silently replace B0.
2. Reacquire installation/operation then bootstrap authority, create a fresh control-only holder and reconciled postboot binding/generation, pin current directory/owner identities and permitted lineage, and take a **fresh** actual original NB lock. Recheck the complete conjunction under held locks and effective launch exclusion. A free lock does not bypass a failed history/audit/authentication check. If an R0 invocation appears, preserve it and refuse without signalling. If old lock/root identity changed unexpectedly across reboot, refuse rather than create a replacement.
3. The new postboot holder, attempt/generation and descriptor generation preserve the original durable operation identity and binding lineage. Do not mutate the preboot immutable Binding/Request; record its reconciled successor and authorized manager/boot changes before issuing requests under it. No preboot challenge, process birth, timestamp, peer, Seal, StartReference, `VerifiedEvidence` or lock custody survives. Run W's issuer barriers, independent checks and one-use consumption afresh; all timestamps are in the current issuer's clock domain.
4. Only if every predicate is positively established may `admit_stopped_r0` return HELD for **this** holder and boundary. Then retain D's work fences, offline predecessor-compatible backup, sequential migration/helper custody, candidate registration/claim-before-DB, readiness, stopped rollback, detach and explicit terminal release ordering. This packet supplies none of those native proofs. No old-R0 temporary daemon, fabricated marker/stop receipt, PID fallback or frozen-bridge change.

The all-boot “no invocation” check has an endpoint: it covers through stopped admission and the old-action suppression boundary. A later deliberately armed, authenticated R1 candidate may use the same service name under D's candidate-ticket-only phase. That launch is not retrospective evidence about R0; it requires its own source/incarnation/alias checks. Never reapply “no astrid.service invocation ever this boot” to that known admitted candidate and never reinterpret a pre-admission R0 launch as the candidate.

### C. Races, cleanup, rollback, and tamper

Maintain the persistent gate until the already specified authorized candidate-only relaxation or terminal cleanup. Restart of the manager, loss of a producer/verifier channel, reload/definition drift, missing active object, changed source/selection/root, new routes, queued old start or audit coverage loss invalidates unconsumed observations. Before effects, refuse; after effects, persist recovery references and retain all available safe custody/gates. A read-only snapshot or pair of barrier timestamps cannot establish uninterrupted launch exclusion.

Create-only publication solves **creation**, not conditional removal/overwrite or manager-setting CAS. `stat → compare → unlink`, even with a retained file descriptor, can unlink a replacement at the pathname. Do not call that replacement-safe. Never unlink/rename/rewrite a changed foreign object to restore the expected view. Safe conditional cleanup needs a reviewed ownership/exclusion mechanism covering the actual parent/object and independent editors through remove/readback. Without it, leave the operation unfinished for explicit recovery rather than claim release or exact rollback. A private directory's ownership is an assumption to verify, not proof that same-user independent editors cannot replace its contents.

After admission, rollback restores old stopped state, exact policy and data under suppression. Reinstall/read back independent denial before any unguarded old action. Record terminal outcome with release_pending, close writers/aliases as D requires, then remove only owned gates with safe removal and native final readback. A racing subsequent authorized R0 launch after the terminal removal boundary is reconciled as a subsequent launch, never exact stopped restoration. No automatic old restart, kill of a replacement or early success. Pre-admission abandonment likewise preserves a pending owned gate until a separately safe cleanup/explicit recovery decision; it must not label the original state restored from a stale snapshot.
