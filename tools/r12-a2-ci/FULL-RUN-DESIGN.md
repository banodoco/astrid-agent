# A2 groups 019–052: bounded workflow draft

`.github/workflows/r12-a2-full.yml` is a review draft, not run authorization. It listens only for first creation of `scratch/r12-a2-full-019-052-20261111` in the specified repository, requires zero before-SHA, no forced push/deletion, first attempt, and has no dispatch event or automatic retry. The triggering ref must be verified absent immediately before any separately coordinated push. It has not been created by this preparation. The old `full-run-workflow.yml.draft` is obsolete. Preserve existing 001–018 evidence and open 005, 015 and 017 potential regressions; no A2 gate is reclassified.

Four independent three-range chains create at most 12 ubuntu-24.04 jobs (019–030, 031–041, 042–052), with at most one range per side and thus at most four concurrent jobs. Each range calls a fixed same-commit reusable workflow; the next range requires the previous range’s explicit successful receipt checkpoint, so an incomplete group or upload failure stops that side. Each job has a 360-minute ceiling: at most 4,320 runner-minutes (72 runner-hours) plus provider rounding for the complete attempt. A job processes its fixed groups in order; each gets a fresh checkout, venv, home, temp, cache and basetemp, the exact four-side pins and frozen manifest/IDs, unchanged pytest selectors and test timeouts, and sanitized per-group receipt upload before the next group. Each artifact uses a literal fixed slot path; a separate post-test sealer reconstructs and validates the exact receipt against frozen IDs, metadata and private captured evidence before allowing upload. Unknown keys, types, hashes or mismatches fail closed. The test-visible driver and runner receive only an explicit environment allowlist via `exec env -i`; GITHUB_OUTPUT and Actions runtime tokens are excluded. No-results entries are explicit. Test failures with complete ID coverage are retained and processing continues. A fixed failure flag passes between completed ranges; the final range fails the side’s workflow if any fully accounted test failed. Earlier range jobs may show green despite accounted failing tests, which remain explicit in their receipts and stage output; no release gate follows from job color. Setup, incomplete coverage, residual-process detection, missing receipt or upload failure stops later groups on that job. Only a successful driver step may enter the sealer; incomplete groups can have no uploaded receipt and remain explicit gaps. Other matrix jobs can continue.

Before and after each group, the driver snapshots same-UID Linux process IDs with start times; any newly surviving process or census error fails that group closed, suppresses its receipt upload and stops the job. This detects observed process residue but cannot prove a fresh VM or exclude process races, detached activity after the snapshot, kernel or external state, or all filesystem and network effects. Groups in a job share a VM and system Python; per-group directories and process sessions do not make them equivalent to independent runners or the prior host. A detached process appearing after the census could race the sealer or tamper with same-UID private evidence; same-UID tests do not provide a strong adversarial isolation boundary. A failed or unverified census makes the driver step fail and prevents the sealer and upload from running. These limits require review before qualifying evidence. The job ceiling or cancellation can interrupt a running group; that group's receipt may be absent or incomplete and any rerun requires separate coordination. Already uploaded artifacts survive subject to service availability and 90-day retention. Failure messages and raw logs are intentionally excluded; sanitized receipts alone cannot establish exact failure-message equivalence or the A2 gate.

Historical timing is insufficient for a useful runtime forecast: for 019–030, the manifest contains 169.09 seconds of known per-side test durations but 362–949 unknown IDs per side; for 031–052 it records zero known duration and 1,664–1,774 unknown IDs per side. Setup repeats per group, and host speeds and queues differ. Therefore 360 minutes may be too short and a full run may leave gaps. Actual available GitHub Actions free quota, billing period, runner multiplier, capacity and provider rounding remain unknown; read-only billing endpoints were unavailable to current credentials. Before any proposed execution, verify authoritative current quota and ensure the permitted unpaid scope covers the agreed worst-case bound including rounding. Do not incur paid usage on an assumption. The bounds do not reserve capacity or promise completion.

Pinned Actions use contents-read permissions and no persisted checkout credentials; group subprocess environments do not receive secrets. Connector/artifact service availability is not established by source review, and a failed upload must block subsequent groups. The full-run workflow, reusable-workflow output propagation, failure markers, upload checkpoint behavior, cross-group isolation and quota need independent review and a separately authorized trigger; local guard tests alone are not a live service proof.
